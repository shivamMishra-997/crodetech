Questions, answered.

The things clients ask us most.

How long does a website take?
Most projects are completed in 5–7 days. If content or feedback arrives late, the estimated delivery date moves with it.
How does payment work?
A 20% non-refundable advance starts a website project. Full payment is due before you receive the final source files, credentials or website handover. Graphic design starts once the advance is received.
How many revisions are included?
Four free revision rounds. Extra changes cost ₹250 each for websites or ₹100 each for graphic design, unless agreed otherwise.
What do I need to provide?
Your logos, images and text. We share guides, such as the Website Content Guide for Clinics, so you know what to prepare.
Do prices include domain and hosting?
No. Domain, hosting, API keys, payment gateways, plugins and renewals are paid directly by you unless your quotation says otherwise.
What happens after handover?
Websites are handed over as-is. Later changes and security fixes are billed as new work, and maintenance sessions are available at the rates on the pricing page.
